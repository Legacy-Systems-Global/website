A letter from our founders

To our shareholders, our neighbors, and the many people who did not choose us —

Every year I sit down at the same oak desk, in the same quiet office, to tell you plainly how your company is doing. I am not a fancy man. I have driven the same car for thirty years, though not always in the same direction, and never for the same reason twice.

People ask me the secret. There isn't one, and I'd tell you if there were, and then I'd own it. The truth is simpler than the business schools make it. You find a wonderful business — one with a moat so wide the customer can't get out — and you buy the whole thing, and you never, ever sell it. Selling is for people who need money. We are not people who need money. We are the reason money needs us.

Now, I read the papers. Some of them we own. And I know there is a certain kind of person who looks at a company like ours and gets nervous. To that person I say: relax. We have never done anything that wasn't, at the time, in some jurisdiction, technically fine. When the rules changed, we changed jurisdictions. That's not evasion. That's respect for the rules — we take them so seriously we go where they can't reach us and admire them from a distance.

A word on our people. They are the heart of Legacy Systems, and we monitor that heart closely, because a heart that skips a beat is a heart worth understanding. We ask a great deal of them. We ask that they log their hours, mind their colleagues, and treat the PO Box as a member of the family. In return we offer stability, purpose, and a benefits package that vests, God willing, before they do.

I won't pretend this year was without its challenges. A regulator retired. A competitor also retired, more suddenly. "Just" Vitamins had what the lawyers call an "event," and what I call Tuesday. Through it all, our free cash flow did what it always does: it flowed, freely, toward us.

I am often asked what I'm afraid of. Death, mostly — which is why we structured the company so it doesn't apply to us. Legacy Systems has no natural end. It is not a man; it cannot die. It is a system, and systems, well-tended, run forever. That is the gift I hope to leave you. Not wealth. Wealth is temporary. Continuity is forever.

Thank you for your trust. It was never really optional, and I'm grateful for it all the same.

Sincerely, The Founders (names withheld)

Our businesses

Mind Control Press

Reaching more than 400 million readers daily across news, lifestyle, opinion, and "opinion you'll form later." Mind Control Press produces content engineered not merely for engagement, but for belief formation, retention, and gentle repetition.

We don't tell you what to think. We tell you what to think about. The rest tends to follow.

Every press release issued by Legacy Systems is distributed by Mind Control Press. Including this one.

Special Purpose Vehicles

Where structured finance meets structured carbon fiber. Our Special Purpose Vehicles division isolates risk, ring-fences liability, and — on race weekends — travels in excess of 200 miles per hour.

Each vehicle is a bankruptcy-remote entity wholly owned by a holding company wholly owned by a holding company. The livery reflects this. Every driver has signed a waiver longer than the car.

Liquidity Events

Legacy Systems' events division produces liquidity events of every kind: IPOs, acquisitions, retirements, estate settlements, and the occasional wedding. Whatever the occasion, we ensure the champagne is cold.

Full-service. Discreet. Non-refundable. Catering available.

Front Running

Front Running makes footwear and apparel for people who intend to finish ahead. Every shoe is engineered with advance knowledge of where your foot is going next, and gets there first.

Our proprietary cushioning platform responds before you do. Our racing line is tuned for the moment just before the field moves. Whether it's a morning 5K or a full marathon, Front Running keeps you one stride ahead of the pack, and comfortably ahead of anyone who might ask how.

Flagship models: the Arbitrage (daily trainer), the Insider (racing flat), and the Latency (long distance, short notice).

Not to be worn while executing orders on behalf of clients.

“Just” Vitamins

It's "just" vitamins.

A full line of daily supplements, powders, and proprietary blends for the modern body. Not evaluated by any authority currently in a position to evaluate them. Ingredients disclosed on a need-to-know basis. Effects reported as "profound" by a statistically meaningful cohort.

Do not exceed the recommended dose. Do not investigate the recommended dose. Consult your physician; we have.

IC9000

Most career ladders end around IC7. Ours keeps going.

IC9000 is a one-on-one coaching program for individual contributors ready to advance beyond the levels their organizations recognize. Through personalized goal-setting, continuous output tracking, and our proprietary leveling framework, participants maximize work and personal output across every hour of the day, including several they didn't know they had.

Every client is assigned a coach, a level, and a recalibration schedule. Levels are assigned, not earned. Progress is shared with your coach, your manager, and IC9000.

Enrollment by referral only. Inquiries may be submitted in writing to our PO Box.
